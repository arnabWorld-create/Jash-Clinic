# IMAGE SHOOT LIST
The supplied phone snapshots are NOT final images. Every slot below is a marked placeholder until a proper photo replaces it. Each image needs descriptive alt text (only what is visible), `next/image`, and a blur placeholder.

| # | Slot | Ratio | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero portrait | 4:5 | Doctor in white coat, plain or softly blurred clean background, no desk clutter, no visible patient records |
| 2 | About portrait | 4:5 | Different pose from #1, same clean-background rules |
| 3 | OPD / consultation room | 3:2 | Tidy, well lit, no readable files |
| 4 | Corridor / reception | 3:2 | Clean, no gate grille, no signage clutter |
| 5 | Waiting area | 3:2 | Well lit, uncluttered |
| 6 | OT / ward | 3:2 | Only if the hospital allows it and the facility is confirmed |

## Rules
- Never show readable patient files, names, prescriptions or screens in any photo
- No before/after imagery, no patient faces without written consent (and none are planned)
- No glass reflections or glare; shoot in daylight or with soft light
- Provide originals at 2400px on the long edge
